About Me

Hi! I am a Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at ShanghaiTech University, where I am in the inaugural AI Honors Program and expect to graduate in June 2027. I research embodied AI safety with Prof. Yuqi Chen at AS3R Lab.

After my Spring 2026 exchange at UC Berkeley, I stayed for summer research. At ARPL, I worked with Prof. Giuseppe Loianno and Ph.D. student Yang Zhou on M3GD for camera–LiDAR novel view synthesis. At ICON Lab, I worked with Prof. Negar Mehr and Ph.D. student Seoyeon Choi on HuGo for LLM-generated humanoid loco-manipulation policies.

I aspire to become a full-stack robotics engineer and scientist (in the near future). I am seeking M.S. or Ph.D. opportunities in robotics and embodied AI for Fall 2027.

Research Interests

  • Embodied AI, Robot Learning, and Robot Perception

As AI transforms the digital world, I hope to bring its capabilities into the physical world through robotics. I am interested in connecting perception and control through learning, enabling robots to adapt through interaction with their surroundings and collaborate on shared tasks.
